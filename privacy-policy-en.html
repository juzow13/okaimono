Privacy Policy

The app "Shopping List" (the "App") is provided by an individual developer. This page explains how information is handled in the App.

Your Lists

The shops and items you enter in the App are stored only on your device and are never sent to any external server. The App has no account registration or login.

The copy and paste features read from or write to the clipboard only when you use them yourself.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to show ads and manage the paid plan. The information sent to them does not include the contents of your lists. The App does not collect information that personally identifies you, such as your name or email address.

  • Google AdMob (advertising): device information, approximate location, ad usage such as impressions and clicks, and on Android, the advertising ID. Ads are not shown to Ad-Free subscribers.
  • RevenueCat (managing the "Ad-Free" paid plan): purchase information (plan and expiration) and an anonymous app user identifier.

AdMob is a Google service, and the information it collects is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Information handled by RevenueCat is subject to the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Payments themselves are processed by Apple (or Google on Android); the App never receives your payment information.

Tracking

The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) on iOS, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used on iOS.

Opting Out and Deletion

To limit ad-related data collection, turn off ad personalization in your device settings. Uninstalling the App deletes all lists stored on your device.

Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

For questions or feedback about the App, please use this form. What you enter in the form and your email address (optional) are used only to reply to you and to improve the App.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page.
